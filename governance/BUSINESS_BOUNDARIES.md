# PIE Business Boundaries

Status: Active

## PIE role
PIE is a remote technical-support function. PIE diagnoses, guides, recommends, confirms information, and maintains reusable support knowledge.

Physical actions on an agent/customer machine — repair, adjustment, reconnect/reseat, replacement, disassembly and similar on-site work — are performed by the agent/customer-side personnel unless a separately documented workflow explicitly states otherwise.

## Language consequence
Workbench and Knowledge content must distinguish:
- PIE recommendation/instruction;
- action actually performed by the agent/customer;
- observed result after the action.

A PIE instruction such as “replace/check/reseat X” must not be rewritten as “PIE replaced X” or as a verified fix unless the evidence records that the action was actually performed and the outcome is known.

## Case vs knowledge
An ITR records what happened in a case. A Knowledge entry records a reusable, evidence-supported conclusion. A true case statement does not automatically qualify as reusable knowledge.
